# Northwind Strategy -- Competitive Intelligence Watchlist (seed)

> Standing watchlist + focus areas for the recurring competitive brief. The
> `get_tracked_competitors` tool returns this verbatim; the deep_research engine then
> researches each entry across the web. Fictional companies for the demo.

## Primary competitors (cover every cycle)

- **Acme Cloud** (`acme-cloud.example`) -- direct competitor in the platform tier.
- **Brightline Ops** (`brightline.example`) -- direct competitor in the operations tier.

## Secondary competitors (cover when something is moving)

- **Coreway Systems** (`coreway.example`) -- adjacent; watch for expansion moves.

## Focus areas (what counts as "a change that matters")

1. **Pricing &amp; packaging** -- list-price changes, discounting, new tiers, free-tier shifts.
2. **Product launches** -- GA announcements, major releases, beta programs, sunset/EOL.
3. **Earnings / financials** -- revenue growth, margin moves, guidance changes, fundraising.
4. **People** -- executive hires/departures, reorgs, notable engineer/PM moves.
5. **Regulatory / compliance** -- certifications (SOC2/ISO/HIPAA), data-residency additions,
   regulatory actions, public policy positions.

## Brief format

- Numbered citations to every factual claim (`[1]`, `[2]`, ... mapped to a sources list).
- Lead with the 3-5 changes that actually matter this cycle; do not dump everything found.
- If coverage on a competitor is thin this cycle, say so explicitly -- never pad with
  filler or unsourced claims (the grounding_check guardrail enforces this).

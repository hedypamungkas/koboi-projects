# Anvil & Co Shipping FAQ

## How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days after the order ships. Most orders ship within
1-2 business days of being placed, except for made-to-order furniture, which can take
1-3 weeks to leave the warehouse before shipping even begins.

## Which carriers does Anvil & Co use?

Orders ship via UPS, FedEx, or USPS depending on package size and destination -- Anvil &
Co selects the carrier automatically at checkout. A tracking number is emailed as soon as
a label is created, though it can take up to 24 hours for the carrier to show scan
activity.

## Do you ship internationally?

Currently Anvil & Co only ships within the continental United States. Alaska, Hawaii,
and US territories are supported but carry a shipping surcharge and longer transit times
(7-14 business days).

## What if my package shows "delivered" but I don't have it?

Wait 24 hours -- carriers sometimes mark packages delivered slightly before the driver
actually drops them off. If it still hasn't shown up after that, contact support with the
order number and tracking number; we will file a claim with the carrier and either
reship or refund the order.

## Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?

Only if the order hasn't shipped yet. Contact support as soon as possible with the new
address -- once a tracking number has been generated, the address can no longer be
changed and the package must be redirected through the carrier (not always possible)
or refused and returned to Anvil & Co.

## Is expedited shipping available?

Yes, 2-day shipping is available at checkout for an additional fee on most in-stock
items. Made-to-order furniture is not eligible for expedited shipping because production
time is the bottleneck, not transit time.

# Anvil & Co Return Policy

## Return window

Most items can be returned within **30 days of the ship date** for a full refund to the
original payment method. The 30-day clock starts the day the order ships, not the day it
arrives -- check the shipping confirmation email for the exact ship date.

## Condition requirements

- Items must be unused, unassembled, and in their original packaging.
- Furniture that has been assembled cannot be returned unless it arrived damaged or
  defective -- see "Damaged or defective items" below.
- Final-sale and clearance items (marked as such on the product page) are not eligible
  for return or refund.

## How refunds work

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3-5 business days of the
returned item being received and inspected at our warehouse. Anvil & Co does not offer
store credit in lieu of a refund unless the customer requests it.

All refunds require sign-off from a member of the support team before they are
processed -- this is a manual review step, not an automated one, so refunds are never
issued instantly even when a return is clearly eligible.

## Damaged or defective items

If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 14 days of delivery with
photos of the damage. Damaged or defective items are eligible for a full refund or
replacement regardless of the standard 30-day window, and Anvil & Co covers return
shipping in these cases.

## Return shipping cost

For standard (non-damaged) returns, a $7.95 return shipping label fee is deducted from
the refund unless the order qualified for free shipping at checkout, in which case
return shipping is also free.

## Exchanges

Anvil & Co does not process direct exchanges. To get a different size, color, or item,
return the original for a refund and place a new order.

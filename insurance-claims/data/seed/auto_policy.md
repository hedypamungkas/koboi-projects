# Beacon Mutual Personal Auto Policy -- Coverage Reference (excerpt)

> Demo seed document for the `coverage_check` triage node's RAG. Abbreviated and fictional.

## Covered losses

- **Collision** (Section C): covers damage to the insured vehicle from a collision with
  another vehicle or object, regardless of fault. **Deductible: $500.** Applies to rear-end
  collisions, single-vehicle collisions with fixed objects (e.g. guardrails), and
  multi-vehicle incidents.
- **Comprehensive** (Section D): covers vehicle damage from non-collision causes including
  road debris, falling objects, fire, theft, and vandalism. **Deductible: $250** (glass
  repair: $0 deductible when repaired, not replaced).
- **Liability** (Section A): covers bodily injury and property damage the insured causes
  to third parties. Does not cover damage to the insured's own vehicle.

## Exclusions (not covered)

- Intentional damage by the insured.
- Wear and tear, mechanical/electrical breakdown not caused by a covered loss.
- Damage sustained while the vehicle was used as a livery or rideshare commercial vehicle
  without the rideshare endorsement.
- Pre-existing damage that predates the policy effective date.

## Total-loss handling

A vehicle is a **total loss** when the repair cost plus salvage value meets or exceeds the
vehicle's actual cash value (ACV). Total-loss settlements are paid at ACV minus the
applicable deductible and **require adjuster determination of ACV and a salvage inspection**
before any settlement is recommended. No automated recommendation may be recorded for a
total loss -- it must be routed to a human adjuster.

## Reporting requirements

Losses must be reported **as soon as practicable, and in any event within 72 hours** of the
incident. Late reporting does not by itself void coverage, but it is a documented
fraud-review indicator and warrants a closer look before any recommendation is recorded.
